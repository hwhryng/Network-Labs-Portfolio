# Lab 01: Multi-Area OSPF (IPv6)

**Purpose:**  
Configure a multi-area OSPF network with IPv6, connecting multiple routers and computers, to build on prior single-area OSPF experience and practice troubleshooting and inter-area communication.

**Background:**  
Refreshed knowledge of OSPF and IPv6 addressing, and learned how to organize and visualize multiple areas in Packet Tracer.

**Key Outcomes:**  
- Configured 9 networks, 5 routers, and 5 computers across multiple OSPF areas  
- Enabled communication between devices in different areas  
- Strengthened problem-solving skills and patience in troubleshooting configuration issues  
- Gained familiarity with Packet Tracer visual organization tools, including labeling and area markers  
- Learned differences between configuring IPv4 and IPv6 networks
