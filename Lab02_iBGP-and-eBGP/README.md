# Lab 02: iBGP and eBGP (IPv4 & IPv6)

**Purpose:**  
Explore iBGP and eBGP configurations, including loopback-based peering, and learn to connect BGP areas to each other in both IPv4 and IPv6.

**Background:**  
Practiced managing multiple routing domains and maintaining session stability using physical and loopback interfaces.

**Key Outcomes:**  
- Configured iBGP and eBGP across multiple routers in IPv4 and IPv6  
- Learned differences and similarities between IPv4 and IPv6 BGP setups  
- Strengthened attention to detail in command entry and troubleshooting  
- Gained experience documenting commands and routing configurations  
- Collaborated with peers to solve complex network problems
